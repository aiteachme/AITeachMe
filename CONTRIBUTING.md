# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve AITeachMe.

## License Of Contributions

Unless a separate written agreement says otherwise, any contribution submitted
to this repository is provided under the same license as the project:
`AGPL-3.0-only`.

You must have the right to submit your contribution, and your contribution must
not include code, data, documentation, model output, images, or other material
that cannot legally be distributed under AGPL-3.0-only.

## Commercial Licensing Flexibility

AITeachMe may offer separate commercial licenses or commercial modules in the
future. For larger contributions, maintainers may require a Contributor License
Agreement before merging so that the project can preserve clear licensing and
commercial authorization paths.

## Generated Files

Do not manually edit generated files such as `frontend/src/api/generated/`.
Regenerate them from the source contract instead.

## Private Data

Do not submit real API keys, private deployment addresses, local absolute
paths, user data, proprietary course material, or private model/provider
configuration.
